# Beta feedback (about 3 minutes)

Thank you for trying BridgeLogics Product Discovery Coach. Please do not include confidential details about your product here.

1. **Ease of use.** How easy was it to get started and choose a path? (1 = hard, 5 = very easy)
2. **Relevance of questions.** Did the questions fit what you had already built? (1 = not at all, 5 = very well)
3. **Quality of feedback.** How useful was the feedback? (1 = not useful, 5 = very useful)
4. **New insight.** Did it point out anything you had not already considered? (Yes / Somewhat / No) If yes, what?
5. **Final output.** Would you use or share the final review? (Yes / Maybe / No)
6. **Generic or specific?** Did it feel generic at any point? Where?
7. **Recommend.** How likely are you to recommend it to someone building a product? (0 to 10)
8. **Improve.** What one change would make it more useful?

Suggested setup: a free Google Form with these eight questions, or reply with answers in a message.
